## Part A — Platform Overview

### 1. Platform Structure

The platform is a unified digital ecosystem for discovering, exploring, documenting and preserving India's rich heritage and culture.

The website is structured around three major experiences:

- **Home Page** — the main landing and discovery experience.
- **Expandable Explore Dashboard** — provides access to deeper sections of the platform.
- **Persistent AI Cultural Assistant** — available throughout the platform.

### 2. Structural Design Principles

The website should be:

- Modular
- Responsive
- Accessible
- Multilingual
- Visually rich
- Source-aware
- Searchable
- Map-driven
- Community-enabled
- AI-ready

The information architecture must remain simple even though the underlying data ecosystem is large.

## Part C — Core Surfaces

### 4. Home Page Structure

The Home Page is the primary user-facing page.

It should not attempt to display every available feature. It should act as a curated entry point into the entire cultural ecosystem.

#### 4.1 Header

The header contains:

- Ministry/platform branding
- Home
- Search access
- Language selector
- Login / Sign Up
- Explore Dashboard trigger
- Accessibility controls (where appropriate)

The design should be modern while maintaining an official Government of India cultural identity.

#### 4.2 Culture Applications and Heritage Showcase

The first major visual section contains a sliding carousel.

The carousel can showcase:

- Government cultural applications
- Indian Culture Portal
- Cultural digital platforms
- Featured heritage sites
- Featured museums
- Featured cultural collections
- Featured traditions

Each card should contain:

- Image
- Title
- Short description
- Category
- Source
- Action button

#### 4.3 Heritage Visual Section

A visual section highlights selected heritage and cultural resources.

Possible content:

- Monuments
- Forts
- Temples
- Museums
- Archaeological sites
- Art forms
- Handicrafts
- Manuscripts
- Cultural landscapes

The section should be image-focused.

#### 4.4 Latest Announcements

A compact horizontal ticker displays recent official announcements.

- The ticker should remain visually small and should not dominate the Home Page.
- Each announcement should be clickable.

#### 4.5 Current and Upcoming Cultural Activities

This section contains event cards.

Possible categories:

- Festivals
- Cultural rituals
- Exhibitions
- Performances
- Workshops
- Museum events
- Cultural celebrations

- Cards should contain date and location information.
- The section should support horizontal sliding.

#### 4.6 About Ministry

The Home Page includes a compact Ministry introduction.

It may contain:

- Minister
- Minister of State
- Ministry introduction
- Organization structure
- Important responsibilities

A button can open the complete About Us section.

#### 4.7 Important Links

Important government and cultural links are grouped here.

#### 4.8 Research Links

Research-oriented resources can include:

- Libraries
- Archives
- Digital collections
- Research institutions
- Cultural catalogues
- Publications
- Institutional resources

#### 4.9 Q&A

A compact question-and-answer area provides common questions and quick access to the AI Cultural Assistant.

#### 4.10 Footer

The footer contains:

- Government links
- Important cultural institutions
- Social media
- Contact information
- Accessibility information
- Terms
- Privacy
- Source information

### 5. Explore Dashboard Structure

The Explore Dashboard is a right-side expandable navigation system.

- It should initially remain compact.
- When opened, it shows the top-level categories:

```
EXPLORE
│
├── About Us
├── Heritage & Culture
├── Offerings
├── Documents
├── Publications & Authors
├── MoUs
└── Media
```

- Selecting a main category expands the next level.
- The interface should use progressive disclosure.

Example:

```
Explore
  ↓
Heritage & Culture
  ↓
India Map
  ↓
Maharashtra
  ↓
Pune
  ↓
Heritage Sites
  ↓
Specific Heritage Site
```

The dashboard should not force users to navigate through multiple unrelated pages.

### 6. AI Cultural Assistant

The AI Assistant is outside the normal navigation hierarchy.

- It is a persistent global feature.
- It should appear as a floating button and can be opened from any page.

It should be capable of answering questions about:

- Heritage
- Culture
- History
- Festivals
- Places
- People
- Books
- Authors
- Documents
- Schemes
- Awards
- Events
- Institutions

It should provide source references whenever possible.

## Part D — Content Sections

### 7. About Us

About Us contains official information about the Ministry and its organizational ecosystem.

- **Mission** — official Ministry mission and purpose.
- **Presentation** — visual presentation of the Ministry.
- **Objectives** — high-level objectives with expandable detailed information.
- **Functions** — responsibilities and functions of the Ministry.
- **Our Team** — leadership and relevant organizational information.
- **Supporting Bodies** — organizations associated with the Ministry.

Possible categories of supporting bodies:

- Attached organizations
- Subordinate organizations
- Autonomous organizations
- Societies
- Libraries
- Research institutions
- Cultural institutions
- Other associated bodies

Each organization may have its own detail page.

### 8. Heritage & Culture Preservation

This is the main discovery section of the platform.

The structure is geographically driven:

```
India
 ↓
State
 ↓
City / District
 ↓
Cultural Category
 ↓
Resource
```

The platform should support both browsing and searching.

### 9. State-Level Structure

Every state has a state landing page.

Possible sections:

- Overview
- History
- Heritage Sites
- Culture
- Rituals & Traditions
- Handloom / Handicrafts
- Famous Food
- Festivals
- Iconic Battles

However, categories must be dynamic. If a category has no meaningful data, it must not appear.

### 10. City / District Structure

Each city or district can contain the same category structure.

Example:

```
Pune
├── History
├── Heritage Sites
├── Culture
├── Festivals
├── Food
└── Handicrafts
```

An unavailable category is hidden.

### 11. Heritage Detail Structure

A detailed heritage page can contain:

- Name
- Images
- Location
- Coordinates
- Historical period
- History
- Cultural significance
- Architecture
- Associated personalities
- Associated historical events
- Related places
- Related documents
- Related publications
- Official sources
- User experiences
- Map

### 12. Offerings Structure

Offerings contain Ministry schemes, awards and commemorations.

**Schemes** include:

- TCC
- Financial assistance
- Anniversary schemes
- Yojanas
- Other Ministry schemes

**Awards** include:

- Gandhi Peace Award
- Tagore Award
- Other cultural awards

Historical years should remain accessible.

**Commemoration** includes:

- Important personalities
- Major contributions
- Historic achievements
- Commemorative events

### 13. Documents Structure

Documents are organized into:

```
Documents
├── Reports
├── Acts & Policies
└── Orders & Notices
```

**Reports** can include:

- Annual reports
- Performance reports
- Budget documents
- Expenditure information
- Cultural institution reports
- Research reports
- Surveys
- Programme reports

**Acts and policies** can include:

- Acts
- Amendments
- Rules
- Regulations
- Policies
- Notifications

**Orders and notices** can include:

- Government orders
- Circulars
- Notifications
- Official notices

### 14. Publications & Authors

Publications are connected to authors and cultural subjects.

**Publication categories:**

- Famous books
- Lesser-known books
- Research publications
- Cultural publications
- Digital publications

**Author categories:**

- Famous authors
- Lesser-known authors
- Researchers
- Cultural scholars

The structure should allow:

```
Author
 ↓
Publication
 ↓
Subject
 ↓
Cultural Resource
```

### 15. MoUs

MoUs are organized by:

- Year
- Organization
- Institution
- Subject
- Partnership type

Examples include ASK, MAKAIAS and other Ministry partnerships.

Historical MoUs should remain searchable.

### 16. Media

Media is the community layer.

It contains:

```
Media
├── Community Profiles
├── User Contributions
├── Community Feed
└── Events
```

**Community profiles** contain:

- Profile image
- Name
- Biography
- Cultural interests
- Contributions

**User contributions** contain:

- Photographs
- Experiences
- Reviews
- Stories

**The community feed** contains:

- Posts
- Photos
- Experiences
- Reviews
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves

**Events** contain:

- Festivals
- Exhibitions
- Performances
- Workshops
- Cultural programmes

**Mobile:**

- Explore Dashboard becomes a drawer/bottom-sheet/floating menu.
- Content becomes vertically stacked.
- Carousels remain swipeable.
- AI Assistant remains a floating button.
- Map becomes touch-friendly.
- Large images are optimized for low bandwidth.
